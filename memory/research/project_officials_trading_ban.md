**Governing thought proposed:** Indonesia has no rule that stops a sitting official from trading
listed shares; the fix is a conflict-of-holdings regime (trading ban + divest-or-qualified-blind-trust
above a threshold + 30-day transaction reports), not a bare trading ban.

**Facts found (desk, 31 Oct 2026, still to verify at T1):**

  Eggers & Hainmueller (2013), Belmont et al. (2022): members on average underperform index.
