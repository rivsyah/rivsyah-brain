**Governing thought proposed:** Indonesia has no rule that stops a sitting official from trading
listed shares; the fix is a conflict-of-holdings regime (trading ban + divest-or-qualified-blind-trust
above a threshold + 30-day transaction reports), not a bare trading ban.

**Facts found (desk, 31 Oct 2026, still to verify at T1):**
- US: Stop Insider Trading Act H.R. 7008 passed House 232-198 on 22 Jul 2026 (bans new buys only);
  Senate cloture failed 53-47 on 30 Sep 2026 per one tracker (other tracker: no vote) — conflict to adjudicate.
- Wei & Zhou, NBER WP 34524 (2025): leaders outperform peers ~47 pp/yr after ascension (n=20 traders).
  Eggers & Hainmueller (2013), Belmont et al. (2022): members on average underperform index.
- IDX/KSEI began >1% shareholder lists 3 Mar 2026 (MSCI request). As of 27 Feb 2026 data: Maruarar
  Sirait >1% in BOLA, COCO, CARS, TRIM; Trenggono one holding (CNBC Indonesia, Bisnis, 5 Mar 2026).
- Tempo: >200 DPR members had not filed LHKPN. KPK (Johanis Tanak) proposes forfeiture of unreported assets.

**Biggest risk to thesis:** Indonesian problem may be controlling business ownership, not trading;
no periodic transaction data exist, so Indonesian trading returns cannot be measured (gap, not a number).
