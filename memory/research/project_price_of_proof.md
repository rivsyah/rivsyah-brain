**The Price of Proof: Verification as Indonesia's Binding Constraint, 2026–2034**, built
28 July 2026 at `Downloads\DEI\Green Economy\Lestari Advisors\New Paper`. 37 pp A4, byline
Rivaldo Harviansyah, same design system as [[project-lestari-advisors]] and
[[project-china-outlook-brief]].

**It is a sibling to, not a revision of, *The Delivery Gap*.** Same EIU pack, deliberately
disjoint slice of it: the first paper used the electricity and RUPTL material, this one uses the
carbon market, carbon tax, land enforcement, palm oil and labour-conditions lines the first never
touched. Audience is Indonesian stakeholders generally — ministries, exporters, OJK, associations —
not a job application.

**Two choices Aldo made when asked**, both worth honouring in follow-up work: topic = the
compliance frontier (over land/seizure, just transition, and adaptation/fiscal alternatives), and
language = **English**, not Bahasa Indonesia.

**Thesis:** four buyer-side regimes bite inside fifteen months (CBAM definitive 1 Jan 2026, EUDR
30 Dec 2026, PSPK 1 Jan 2027, EU Forced Labour 14 Dec 2027; UK CBAM 1 Jan 2027). The cost is
deferred — CBAM factor 2.5% in 2026, 100% in 2034 — and the evidence is not, because accredited
verification became a precondition on 1 Jan 2026 and a closed data year cannot be reconstructed.
Framework: **the Evidence Chain** (measure → verify → trace → recognise); recommendations: **the
Proof Agenda** (Instrument / Assure / Claim). Sharpest point: CBAM Article 9 credits a carbon price
*effectively paid*, and Indonesia's is ~1/20th of the EU's, so not raising it decides which
treasury collects.

**Method difference from the earlier briefs:** the evidence base is explicitly two-layered —
Layer 1 the EIU pack (S1–S6), Layer 2 ~40 web-verified public facts keyed A/B/C/D/E/F with a stated
**28 July 2026 verification cutoff**. `sources/dossier.md` holds both plus a cautions list. Repeat
that structure for any future paper mixing a client pack with open sources.
